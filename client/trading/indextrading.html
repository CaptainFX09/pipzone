HOW IT WORKS

Trade Cycle Flow

Your trade cycle follows a simple managed process.

01

Signal

A trade signal is issued for the active trading window.

02

Direction

Select BUY or SELL according to the available signal.

03

Settlement

The cycle is settled by the trading desk after the active window.

04

Result

Your client share is reflected in the settled account balance.

i

Trading Schedule

Forex trading is closed on Saturday and Sunday. No forex trading result is generated on weekends. Daily settlement is processed at midnight Thailand Time.
