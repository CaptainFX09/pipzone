How it works

Every account follows the same process. Trading results are recorded to your account based on actual trading performance.

Common questions

FAQ

What's the minimum deposit?+

$100 in USDT, sent via TRC20 or BEP20, to open an account.

How is the profit split calculated?+

Each day's actual trading result is calculated. You receive 60% and the desk receives 40%.

When can I withdraw?+

Eligible profit shares may be requested for withdrawal according to the applicable withdrawal terms. Your original deposit (principal) can be withdrawn once 40 days have passed since it was credited. There is no minimum withdrawal amount, and a fixed $2 withdrawal fee applies to every request. Once approved, withdrawals are typically received within 5 minutes to 8 hours.

Is my capital guaranteed?+

No. Forex and crypto trading can result in losses.

Do you trade every day?+

We trade forex and crypto on trading days. Forex markets are closed on Saturdays and Sundays, so no trade is placed and no profit/loss is updated on your account those two days. Crypto markets trade every day, but results are still settled and updated on the same daily schedule as forex.

Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 2026

1. The arrangement

PipZoNe ("the desk", "we", "us") uses client-provided capital for forex and crypto trading under an agreed 60/40 profit-sharing structure. Trading results depend on actual market performance and may result in either profits or losses. By creating an account you agree to the terms described here and on the rest of this site.

2. Minimum deposit

The minimum deposit to open an account is $100, sent in USDT via the TRC20 or BEP20 network only. Deposits are credited to your account balance only after the transaction is confirmed on-chain.

3. Profit and loss sharing

Trading results are recorded for the relevant account and allocated according to the agreed 60% client and 40% desk sharing structure. Positive results increase the applicable account balance, while negative results reduce it. The sharing structure does not guarantee a profit or protect against losses.

4. Withdrawals

There is no minimum withdrawal amount. A fixed withdrawal fee of $2 is deducted from every withdrawal request; the remaining amount is paid out to you. Your accumulated profit share may be withdrawn on demand, at any time. Your original deposited capital (principal) may only be withdrawn once 40 days have passed since it was credited to your account. Requests are manually reviewed before approval. Once approved, funds are typically received within 5 minutes to 8 hours, paid to the wallet address you provide.

5. No guaranteed returns

Nothing in these terms, on this site, or in any communication from PipZoNe constitutes a guarantee of profit or of capital preservation. Forex and crypto trading carries a material risk of loss, up to and including the loss of your entire deposited capital.

6. Account accuracy

You are responsible for providing accurate deposit transaction hashes and withdrawal wallet addresses, and for selecting the correct network. PipZoNe is not responsible for funds sent to an incorrect address, on an incorrect network, or with incorrect transaction details.

7. Termination

Either party may close the arrangement. On closure, your remaining available balance is paid out through the standard withdrawal process, subject to the principal lock-in period described above.

8. Referral program

Clients may share a personal referral link. Referral earnings are not activated until the referring client's own first deposit has been approved. No commission is generated by a pending or rejected deposit, by a referred signup on its own, or more than once for the same approved deposit. Self-referral is not permitted, and each referred client is attributed to a single referrer. Commission rates are set by PipZoNe and may be changed at any time; the rate applied to a given deposit is the rate in effect when that deposit is approved.

9. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Continued use of your account after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

When you create an account we collect your full name, email address, phone number, and the wallet address(es) you provide for withdrawals. We also record your deposit and withdrawal requests, the resulting account balance history, and — if you joined via a referral link, or if you refer other clients — your referral relationship and referral earnings history. Risk Disclosure

Last updated: September 2026

Trading risk

Forex and crypto trading is highly speculative and involves a significant risk of loss. Past performance of any trading strategy or account is not indicative of future results. You should only deposit capital you can afford to lose. Forex markets are closed on Saturdays and Sundays, so no trading result is recorded and no profit/loss update happens on those days.

No guarantee of profit

The 60/40 profit split describes how a trading result is divided — it is not a promise of what that result will be. Some days may produce a loss, which is shared in the same proportion as a profit.

Liquidity and withdrawal timing

Your profit share can be requested for withdrawal at any time and is subject to manual review. Your original deposited capital is locked for 40 days from the date it is credited. Once a withdrawal is approved, funds are typically received within 5 minutes to 8 hours. This means your principal is not available on demand in the same way as a bank account.

Not a bank or regulated fund

PipZoNe is a managed trading arrangement between you and the desk. It is not a bank, is not FDIC or similarly insured, and is not a licensed or regulated investment fund. Deposits are not protected by any deposit insurance scheme.

Independent advice

Nothing on this site is financial or investment advice. You are encouraged to seek independent financial advice before depositing capital, and to only invest an amount you are fully prepared to lose.

This page is a plain-language summary for clients and is not a substitute for professional legal advice. If you are operating PipZoNe as a business, have this page (and the Terms & Privacy pages above) reviewed by a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction before relying on them.
