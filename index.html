Capital Protection Policy

Before You Deposit

We believe full transparency builds long-term trader alignment. Understand our liquidity covenants before joining.

Trading Involves Real Risk

Past returns do not guarantee future performance. Foreign exchange and digital assets fluctuate. The desk deploys automated stop-loss thresholds on every trade.

40-Day Principal Lock

To avoid fire-sales and maintain liquidity depth during open swing positions, deposited principal is committed for a 40-day cycle before capital withdrawal.

Profits Withdrawable Anytime

Even while your principal capital is active in the 40-day cycle, all accrued 60% client profits can be withdrawn on demand without penalty. Withdrawals are processed within 5 minutes to 8 hours after approval.

On-Chain USDT Verification

All deposits and withdrawals are processed via transparent blockchain rails (Tether (USDT TRC20) or Tether (USDT BEP20)) with verifiable transaction hashes and immutable timestamps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Direct answers regarding settlements, withdrawals, and risk management.

The minimum deposit is $100 in USDT (available via Tether (USDT TRC20) or Tether (USDT BEP20) networks). There is no upper limit. Larger allocations are managed across segmented liquidity sub-accounts for minimal market slippage.
At the end of each daily settlement cycle, realized net profits generated by our desk trading are calculated. 60% of the net profit is credited directly to your client balance, and 40% is retained by the desk. If there is a flat or negative day, the desk takes $0.
Your profit earnings can be withdrawn anytime, 24/7, subject to withdrawal processing. You will receive your payout within 5 minutes to 8 hours. Your initial deposited principal has a 40-day lock period to allow our execution algorithms to run full strategic cycles without disrupting open margin allocations. After 40 days, principal can be withdrawn or rolled over.
No legitimate financial market trading can guarantee capital. However, PipZoNe operates strict risk controls: hard position stop-losses, a 2% max daily portfolio risk cap, and algorithmic hedging between gold, major currency pairs, and stable crypto pairings.
Forex currency and precious metal markets operate Monday through Friday. Our trading desk is closed on Saturdays and Sundays, and no trading results are generated during the weekend. Trading activity resumes with the Monday market session.
PipZoNe charges $0 deposit fees and a fixed $2 withdrawal fee applies. The same $2 fee applies to withdrawals via Tether (USDT TRC20) and Tether (USDT BEP20).
